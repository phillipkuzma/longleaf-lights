Pricing

Install and takedown are separate visits, each billed on its own. Not sure of your roofline length? Pick your best guess. We measure on install day, and if it's off by more than about 10%, we'll talk before starting.

Hanging and connecting your lights, November and December weekends.

Takedown visit

Light kits

Questions

Do I need to be home?

No. Everything we do is on the outside of your home. Leave an outlet we can reach and tell us about any gate code when you book.

Can I get my deposit back?

Deposits hold dates we turn other people away for, so they're non-refundable if you cancel. The one exception: if your Lift Weekend doesn't reach its minimum, you can move dates or get a full refund.

Which package fits my home?

Packages are based on your front roofline. Most single-story homes are Cottage or Classic. If your home is much larger, or you want the sides and back done too, book the closest package and mention it in your notes. We'll confirm before starting.

Can you use lights I already have?

Yes. Choose "Use my own lights" when you book. They need to be outdoor-rated and working. If a strand fails, we can replace it at cost.

When do the lights come down?

Takedowns run on January weekends. You can book a takedown date when you book your install, or take them down yourself.

How do I pay the rest?

After each visit, we text you a secure Stripe link for that visit's balance. You can pay by card or Apple Pay.
